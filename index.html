a cozy notebook that lives in your browser ✿

Notes, in an actual notebook.

Nook is a notes app shaped like a spiral notebook. Design the cover, turn real pages, tick off to-dos, and stick on sticky notes and photo-booth strips. Everything is encrypted with your password, right on your device.

write like it’s paper

Blocks, to-dos and sticky notes

Type / for any block: headings, lists, to-dos, quotes, callouts and dividers. Markdown shortcuts work too (#, -, []). Drag blocks side by side into columns, and pull sticky notes and containers out of the toolbox to put anywhere on the page.

When a page fills up, your writing carries on to the next one, like it would on paper. Turn pages with the arrows, Alt+←/→ or a swipe.

say cheese

Scrapbook albums, with a photo booth

Album pages come laid out with photo frames, on warm scrapbook paper. Fill them from your computer, or from the built-in booth: one shot or a classic strip of four, with Natural, B&W, Warm, Retro and Cool filters. Add stickers and captions, then reshuffle the layout in one click.

a shelf of your own

A bookcase for all of them

Nook opens on your bookcase. Notebooks and albums stand on shelves you name, each spine in its own cover’s colors. Drag books to rearrange them, and design every cover: color, pattern, sticker and elastic band.

private by design

Locked. Encrypted. Only on your device.

  • Your password becomes an AES-256 key (PBKDF2, 600,000 rounds). It’s never stored and never sent anywhere.
  • Notes and photos are saved sealed, in your browser. There’s no Nook server holding a copy.
  • No account, no sign-up, and no tracking inside the app.
  • Lock Nook with one click, and it stays shut until the password is entered again.

Questions

Where are my notes stored?

In your browser on this device, encrypted with your password. Clearing this site’s data in your browser clears them too, so use Export now and then to keep a backup.

What happens if I forget my password?

There’s no reset. Without the password, your notes can’t be decrypted by anyone. You can erase everything and start fresh, or import a backup you exported earlier.

Can I use Nook on more than one device?

Each browser keeps its own shelf. To move it, export a backup (one .json file, photos included) and import it on the other device. The backup file isn’t password-protected, so keep it somewhere safe.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On small screens the book shows one page at a time, and you swipe to turn.

Does the photo booth upload my pictures?

No. The camera only runs while the booth is open, and your shots are encrypted and saved on your device like everything else.
